**Simple, private financial tracking.**

I've always felt uncomfortable connecting my main bank accounts to third-party budgeting apps. I wanted a place where I could track my finances exactly how I wanted, without worrying about data privacy, broken bank connections, or hidden fees.

That's why I'm building **KeyFinz**. It's a personal finance tool designed for people who want total control over their money. It doesn't guess where your money went—you tell it.

---

### **Why I Built This**

Most finance apps today try to automate everything, but often at the cost of your privacy or accuracy. I wanted to build something different:

- **Privacy First**: I believe your financial data belongs to you. KeyFinz doesn't connect to your bank, so you never have to share your login credentials.
- **Mindful Spending**: There is value in manually tracking your expenses. It makes you more aware of every dollar leaving your wallet.
- **Global Focus**: As someone who deals with different currencies, I needed a tool that handles multi-currency accounts without the headache.

---

### **What You Can Do**

- **Track on Your Terms**: Log your income and expenses manually. It's simple, fast, and ensures your records are 100% accurate.
- **Chat with Your Data**: I've integrated an AI assistant that you can actually talk to. Instead of digging through complex charts, you can just ask: "How much did I spend on groceries last month?" or "Am I on track with my savings?"
- **Manage Debts**: Keep track of money you've lent to friends or borrowed, so nothing slips through the cracks.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Seamlessly manage accounts in different currencies and handle transfers between them effortlessly.
