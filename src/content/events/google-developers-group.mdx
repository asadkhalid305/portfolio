I participated as a mentor at Community Lounge – Day 1 during Google I/O Extended 2024, organized by GDG Kolachi. During the session, I shared insights from my professional experience as a Software Development Engineer, with a focus on web development, practical engineering workflows, and industry expectations.

The session aimed to provide attendees with real-world perspectives on building and scaling web applications, as well as guidance for developers navigating their careers in modern software development ecosystems.
