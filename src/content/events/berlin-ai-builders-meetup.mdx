I had the opportunity to speak at the Berlin AI Builders Meetup, hosted at Ojin AixHaus. The event brought together scientists, machine learning engineers, founders, and AI enthusiasts to discuss how AI is shaping products, platforms, and people.

In my talk, **"Designing Real-World Multi-Agent AI Systems,"** I shared insights into the practical challenges and solutions involved in building robust multi-agent architectures. We explored how these systems can be designed to handle complex, real-world tasks effectively.

The meetup was a fantastic space for hands-on builders and curious minds to exchange ideas and explore the future of AI technology.
