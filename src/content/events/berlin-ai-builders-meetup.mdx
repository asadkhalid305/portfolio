I am excited to deliver a talk to the amazing community at the **Berlin AI Builders Meetup** on what an AI agent is and how multiple agents come together to form a complete system.

There's a lot that happens behind the scenes in multi-agent systems, and in this talk, **"Designing Real-World Multi-Agent AI Systems,"** I’ll break those concepts down step by step, focusing on how they actually work and fit together.

The event will bring together scientists, machine learning engineers, founders, and AI enthusiasts at Ojin AixHaus. I'm looking forward to sharing my knowledge and having a great conversation about how AI is shaping products, platforms, and people.
